Nostr Chinese Checkers

A classic star-board strategy game. Play against the engine, share the board locally, or challenge another player in a public or encrypted private Nostr match.

CHINESE CHECKERS BOARDLocal game · Red to move
Select a marble, then choose a highlighted hole. Move one adjacent space or make a chain of hops over any marble; jumped marbles stay on the board.

Game controls

Rules: each turn moves one marble to an adjacent hole or chains hops over occupied holes. Hopped marbles stay put. First to move all 10 marbles into the opposite triangle wins.
